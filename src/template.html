3 · Train a decision tree

The machine looks for the question that best separates leavers from loyal clients, then repeats inside each group.

Decision tree. The same rules are listed as text in step 4.
Share of clients who left:lowhigh

Why a minimum group size? A rule that covers 2 clients is an anecdote, not a pattern. Anomaly detection (e.g. Isolation Forest) does the opposite: it keeps splitting until one client is isolated, and the fastest isolated are the most unusual.

How is the best level found?

Score = how well the model ranks leavers above loyal clients (50% = coin flip).
